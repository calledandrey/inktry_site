Privacy Policy

InkTry App (the “App”)
Last Updated: December 13, 2025

Thank you for using InkTry (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the App, including AI-powered tattoo image generation and the on-device “Try On” feature.

  • Try On is on-device: photos used for Try On are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded by us.
  • AI generation uses third parties: text prompts and generation parameters are sent to external AI services (via AiProxy and fal.ai).
  • Purchases: subscription/receipt data is handled via Adapty (and the app store platform).
  • Analytics: we use Amplitude to understand UI interactions and improve the App (not to identify you personally).

1. Who We Are (Data Controller)

This App is provided by an individual developer (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”, “our”). Contact details are provided in Section 12.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Identifiers and Account-Related Information

  • App User Identifier: we may assign a pseudonymous identifier (e.g., a random ID) to keep the App functional, support subscriptions, prevent abuse, and maintain continuity across sessions.
  • Device and app identifiers: we may collect device model, OS version, app version, language/region, and technical identifiers necessary for analytics and service integrity.

2.2 Subscription and Purchase Information (Adapty)

If you purchase an auto-renewing subscription or other in-app purchases, we (and/or our purchase provider) process information needed to validate purchases and manage entitlements. This may include:

  • subscription status (active/expired), product identifiers, and entitlement flags;
  • purchase/transaction identifiers and receipt validation results;
  • country/region (derived from the store) and storefront information.

Payment card details are not provided to us. Payments are processed by the relevant app store/platform, and purchase infrastructure is supported via Adapty.

2.3 Analytics and Product Improvement (Amplitude)

We use Amplitude to understand how users interact with the App (for example, which screens are opened, what buttons are tapped, and how often features are used). This data helps us improve usability, performance, and reliability. We aim to keep analytics pseudonymous and focused on product usage. We do not intentionally send your photo content used in Try On to analytics.

2.4 AI Tattoo Image Generation Data

When you generate a tattoo image, the App processes:

  • Text prompts you type (and optional negative prompts if applicable);
  • Style selections and other generation parameters (e.g., aspect ratio, size, guidance/quality parameters, seed if used);
  • Request metadata necessary to operate the generation (e.g., timestamps, error logs, rate-limit counters).

How generation requests are processed: the App sends prompts and generation parameters to AI providers via:

  • AiProxy (secure proxy / abuse prevention layer)
  • fal.ai (model API aggregation / compute layer)

Your generation request may be executed using third-party models, including Google-provided image generation models made available through fal.ai or its partners. We do not operate or own the underlying AI models.

2.5 “Try On” Feature (On-Device Processing)

The App’s “Try On” feature overlays a generated tattoo on top of a photo or live camera view using on-device rendering and gestures. Photos used for Try On are processed locally on your device and are not sent to our servers.

  • If you choose a photo from your library or take a photo, the App uses it only to render the overlay on your device.
  • Any saved “Try On” results (if you choose to save/export) are stored in your device storage and are controlled by you.

2.6 User-Generated Content Stored in the App

The App may store the following on your device to provide features like history and quick access:

  • Generated images (tattoo outputs)
  • Generation history (e.g., prompt text, style, date/time, and settings)
  • Saved prompts (if the App provides a prompt library)

Unless you explicitly use a cloud sync feature provided by your platform (e.g., iCloud/device backups), this content is stored locally.

2.7 Log and Security Data

Like most online services, requests to AI services may involve standard log data such as IP address, request timestamps, device/app versions, and error diagnostics. This data may be processed by our service providers (AiProxy / fal.ai) to operate the service, mitigate abuse, and debug issues.

3. How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • Provide core functionality: generate tattoo images, show history, and enable Try On overlays.
  • Process subscriptions: verify purchases and enable premium features via Adapty/platform receipts.
  • App improvement: analyze feature usage (Amplitude) to improve UX and stability.
  • Security and abuse prevention: detect fraud/abuse and protect API usage (AiProxy / fal.ai).
  • Customer support: respond to user requests and troubleshoot issues.

4. Legal Bases (EEA/UK and Similar Jurisdictions)

Where required by law (e.g., GDPR), we process personal data under one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Contract necessity: to provide the App and requested features (e.g., generating images, subscription access).
  • Legitimate interests: to improve the App, maintain security, prevent abuse, and debug problems.
  • Consent: where required (e.g., certain analytics controls), which you may withdraw as described below.
  • Legal obligations: to comply with applicable laws and enforce our rights.

7.3 Analytics Retention

Analytics data is retained according to our configuration and Amplitude’s policies and is used to improve the App.
